Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my service?

Once your payment is confirmed on the blockchain via U.CASH, our Cloudflare Webhook instantly wakes up the AI agent in GitHub Actions. The AI processes your request and saves the result in the deliveries/ folder of this repository.

How does the Claim Code work?

To protect your privacy, we do not use your name or email. When you place an order, you must include a secret code in your prompt, like this: [Claim: your_secret_password]. The bot will save your result using a SHA-256 hash. Only you, knowing your transaction ID and your Claim Code, will be able to find and read your file.

Is my code or data used to train external AIs?

Absolutely not. This agent runs entirely on local PyTorch models (Qwen 3B, Whisper, Tesseract, ViT). We do not use OpenAI, Anthropic, or any paid external API. Your data never leaves the GitHub Actions runner, ensuring maximum privacy.

What if the AI takes a long time?

Inference on local CPU can take a few minutes depending on the complexity of your request (especially the $20 Smart Contract Audit). If your file is not there immediately, wait 5-10 minutes and refresh the deliveries/ folder.

Can I get a refund?

Because the AI immediately consumes compute resources (CPU/RAM) to process your specific request, we do not offer refunds. However, if the AI fails to generate a response, the file will contain an error log, and we will manually review it.
